AHP decision tool

Pairwise comparison of criteria to derive weights, with a consistency check, then options scored either directly or pairwise. Works for any decision with a few criteria and a few alternatives.

1

The decision

State it as a question with a real answer. "Which vendor for the LMS renewal" beats "the LMS thing".

2

Criteria

Three to six works best. Comparisons grow quickly, so six criteria means fifteen judgements.

3

Options

The alternatives being decided between. Two to six.

4

Weight the criteria

For each pair, drag toward the one that matters more, and by how much. Centre means equal. Answer quickly, since deliberation tends to smuggle in the conclusion you already prefer.

Derived weights
5

Score the options

Direct rating is faster and easier to explain in a room. Pairwise is the full method, and it earns its extra time when options are hard to score on an absolute scale, for instance candidates or designs.

6

Result

Nothing is stored in the browser. Use "Save to file" to keep a decision, and "Load from file" to reopen it later.

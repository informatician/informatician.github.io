Consulting and Advisory Practice

My consulting and advisory work focuses on helping organizations leverage technology, data, and innovation to solve strategic challenges and deliver measurable results. Drawing on both academic research and professional experience, I work with leaders and teams to clarify objectives, design evidence-based roadmaps, and translate ideas into implementation.

I believe the most valuable consulting bridges research and practice — transforming evidence and insight into meaningful, measurable change.

Consulting Approach

My approach to consulting emphasizes collaboration, structure, and capability transfer. I use systems thinking to align people, processes, and technology, ensuring that every initiative contributes to long-term organizational resilience. Engagements typically involve structured milestones, iterative feedback, and co-creation with client teams — with a focus on delivering both insight and implementation capacity.

Build a course AI policy from pedagogy first.

Answer six short questions to identify the course-policy archetypes that best fit your teaching context. Then set assessment-level overrides and add the responsibility clauses your course requires.

Core design principleStart with what students need to learn and demonstrate. The appropriate role of generative AI follows from those learning and assessment requirements. Information risk primarily adds guardrails, rather than determining the course archetype by itself.
